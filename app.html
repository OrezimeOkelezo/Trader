TRADINGVIEW DATA

Market data is supplied by TradingView and its data providers. Availability, exchange permissions and delays vary by instrument and region. Confirm quotes with your MT4/MT5 broker before interpreting differences.

MOMENTUM

RSI · Relative Strength Index

Common MT4/MT5 oscillator that measures recent price momentum. Learners often study the 30 and 70 reference zones, while remembering that strong trends can keep RSI elevated or depressed.

Study: momentum and divergence. Not: an automatic reversal call.
TREND / MOMENTUM

MACD

Compares moving averages to explore momentum shifts. The MACD line, signal line and histogram can help explain changes in momentum, but can lag and produce false readings in sideways markets.

Study: line relationship and histogram. Not: a guaranteed entry trigger.
TREND

Moving averages · EMA / SMA

Average prices over a selected period. MT4/MT5 learners commonly compare shorter and longer averages to describe trend direction and dynamic areas of interest.

Study: slope and price context. Not: a forecast.
VOLATILITY

Bollinger Bands

Volatility bands around a moving average. Band expansion and contraction describe changing volatility. Touching a band alone does not mean price must reverse.

Study: volatility and compression. Not: a standalone signal.
MT4 / MT5 COMPATIBILITY

Same analysis concepts, broker-specific prices

RSI, MACD, moving averages and Bollinger Bands are standard indicator concepts available in MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5. TradingView symbols and broker symbols may differ, and spreads, candle close times and prices can vary. Match the exact instrument and timeframe on your broker's demo chart before comparing studies.
