Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Trader educational website handles information when you visit, create an account or use its learning and paper-practice features. Review this notice before creating an account.

1. Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, Trader may handle your name, email address, Supabase account identifier, lesson progress, watchlist preferences, simulated paper-trade records, and messages or settings you choose to submit. The authentication provider also processes information needed to secure your account.

2. Why information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, provide sign-in and password recovery, save learning progress and simulated activity, maintain platform reliability, prevent misuse, and respond to support or privacy requests.

3. Service providers and embedded content

Trader uses Supabase for authentication and database services. The Trading Signals area may load an embedded chart from TradingView. If Telegram notifications are enabled, the notification feature sends the selected notification content to the Telegram account configured by the user. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Their availability and practices are outside Trader's direct control.

4. Cookies, sessions and technical information

Authentication sessions and essential browser storage may be used to keep you signed in and protect account access. Embedded third-party content may use its own technologies. Do not assume that all third-party tracking is controlled by Trader. If optional analytics or advertising are added later, this notice and any required consent choices should be updated first.

5. Sharing

Trader does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers described above, where necessary to operate the service, or disclosed where required by law or to protect users and the platform.

6. Retention and security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect the platform. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also stop using the service. Requests can be made through the support contact published by the service operator.

8. Children and young users

Trader is an educational demonstration and is not intended to collect more personal information than is necessary to provide its features. A parent or guardian should be involved where required by applicable law. Do not submit another person's personal information without permission.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as the platform changes. The latest version and its effective date will be published on this page.

10. Operator contact

Before public launch, the site operator should publish a working privacy contact address and business/contact details here so users can make privacy requests and receive required notices.

This is a starter privacy notice based on the currently inspected website features. It is not legal advice. The operator must verify actual data flows, retention settings, third-party services and contact details before public launch.
